# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Adults looking for a simple, low-pressure way to meet people online. They may be arriving from search, social, or a shared link and need to understand the product quickly before allowing camera and microphone access.

## Product Purpose

1V1Pro helps people start a real one-to-one conversation online. The landing page should make video chat feel approachable, explain that users can switch to text, and make safety and user control visible before the first click.

## Brand Personality

Human, open, reassuring. The voice should feel warm and direct without sounding like a dating ad, a gambling site, or a generic social feed.

## Anti-references

Avoid spammy dating-site language, exaggerated promises, fake social proof, crowded app-store style layouts, and imagery that implies users are real when it is still placeholder media.

## Design Principles

- Show the conversation before explaining the platform.
- Make the user's pace and control obvious.
- Use one clear primary action per section.
- Let real imagery carry emotion once it is available; placeholders must be honest and easy to replace.
- Keep copy specific, calm, and adult-oriented.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.2 AA for text and controls. Preserve visible keyboard focus, use semantic headings and buttons, support reduced motion, keep touch targets at least 44px, and never rely on color alone to communicate status or safety.
