FAQ

Questions, answered

What is online video chat?

Online video chat lets two or more people communicate through live video and audio over the internet. On 1v1chat, the experience is built around meeting people and having real-time one-on-one conversations.

What is 1v1 video chat?

1v1 video chat is a private face-to-face conversation between two people. Instead of joining a group room, you can focus on one person and one conversation at a time.

How do I start a video chat on 1v1chat?

Choose someone you want to talk with, select video chat, and follow the on-screen steps to start your call.

Can I meet people from other countries through video chat?

Yes. 1v1chat is designed for global conversations, so you can discover people from different countries, cultures, and backgrounds.

What can I talk about in a video chat?

Start with something simple: music, travel, hobbies, games, everyday life, or something you notice you have in common. Natural questions usually work better than trying to find the perfect opening line.

Can I start with text before video?

Yes. Text chat can make it easier to break the ice before deciding whether you want to continue face to face on video.
